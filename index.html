Bitbench Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026

Bitbench ("the app") is published by Code Compiler. In short: the app collects no data. It has no internet permission, so nothing you type or paste can leave your phone through the app. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking.

1. Information the app collects

None. Every tool (JSON, JWT, Base64, hashes, regex, cron and the others) runs entirely on your device. The app does not request the Android internet permission, and we (the developer) do not run any server that receives data from it.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Recent inputs: to save you time, each tool can remember your recent inputs. They are saved only on your phone. You can turn this off or clear it at any time in Settings.
  • Never stored: inputs and results of the Password / Secret generator and the Hash & HMAC tool (including HMAC keys) are never saved, not even on your phone.
  • Settings and favourites: your theme and pinned tools are saved on your phone.

Uninstalling the app removes all of this data.

3. Clipboard and sharing

  • The app reads the clipboard only when you tap Paste. It never reads it on its own.
  • When you tap Share, Android's share sheet opens and the text goes only to the app you pick, under your control. Nothing is shared otherwise.

4. Permissions

The app requests no permissions: no internet, location, storage, camera, contacts or notifications. The "Privacy policy" link in Settings opens this page in your own browser.

5. What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, sell, rent or share any information.
  • It does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or files.
  • It contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools.

6. Children

The app is made for developers and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.

7. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
